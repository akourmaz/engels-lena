About your teacher

Hi, I’m Lena — your personal English teacher

I’m a professional English teacher based in the north of the Netherlands, and I teach the way I wish someone had taught me: you talk, I guide. No school desks, no fear of mistakes — a conversation that happens to be a lesson.

Besides teaching, I work with business correspondence — e-mails, offers, official letters. That’s my specialty: my students don’t just speak better English, they write messages that get answers.

My students come from the Netherlands, Belarus, Poland, Ukraine and Russia. If a tricky grammar point needs it, I can switch to Russian — and switch right back to English practice.

MA in English Philology; teaching English to adults and teams.

Frequently asked questions

How much do business English lessons cost?

My prices are open: a single 60-minute lesson is €45, a package of 5 lessons is €215 (€43/hour) and a package of 10 is €400 (€40/hour). The 25-minute trial lesson is free. In-company group training is quoted per team.

Do you teach groups at companies?

Yes — at your workplace in Drenthe and Groningen. Practical sessions built around your team’s real work: meetings, customer contact, e-mails. Send a short message about your team and I’ll reply with a proposal within 24 hours.

Can I learn business English online effectively?

Yes — for working adults it often beats a classroom. You speak for most of the lesson instead of waiting for your turn, we rehearse your real meetings and rewrite your real e-mails, and lessons fit around your work day.

What happens at the trial lesson?

We meet online for 25 minutes: we talk about your goal, I assess your level in a relaxed conversation, and you get a personal learning plan. No obligation — you decide afterwards.
