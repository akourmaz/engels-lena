Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026. This page explains what personal data I collect, why, and what your rights are under the GDPR (AVG).

Who I am

Elena Hagenaar, trading as Engels met Lena, Borger (Drenthe), the Netherlands. Contact: elena.v.hagenaar@gmail.com or 06 1840 4015.

What data I collect and why

Intake call request form: your name, one contact detail of your choice (an e-mail address or a phone/WhatsApp number), and anything you choose to write in the optional message. The form also records which button on the site you came from and — if you used the level check — the level it suggested, so that I do not have to ask you again. I use these only to reply to you and to prepare our lessons. The form is sent from this website to my own mailbox; a copy of the request is stored in a private Google Sheet that only I can open, so that I do not lose anyone’s request (Google acts as a processor — see the Google privacy policy).

WhatsApp: if you choose the WhatsApp button, your message and phone number are processed by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland) under their own privacy policy. I keep the chat to answer you and organise our lessons.

E-mail: if you write to me directly, I keep the correspondence to answer you and organise our lessons.

Analytics: this site uses privacy-first analytics without cookies and without tracking you across websites. No personal profiles are created, which is why the site shows no cookie banner.

What I do not do

I do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing, do not send newsletters without your explicit consent, and do not use advertising trackers.

How long I keep data

Lesson-related data is kept while we work together and up to 2 years afterwards; invoices are kept 7 years as required by Dutch tax law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time — just e-mail elena.v.hagenaar@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
