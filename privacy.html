Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026. This page explains what personal data I collect, why, and what your rights are under the GDPR (AVG).

Who I am

Engels met Lena (sole proprietorship, the Netherlands), KvK 00000000. Contact: hello@engelsmetlena.nl.

What data I collect and why

Trial lesson booking (Calendly): your name, e-mail address and your answers to three short questions (language, goal, level). I use these only to prepare and run your lessons. Calendly processes this data as an external service — see the Calendly privacy policy.

E-mail: if you write to me, I keep the correspondence to answer you and organise our lessons.

Analytics: this site uses privacy-first analytics without cookies and without tracking you across websites. No personal profiles are created, which is why the site shows no cookie banner.

What I do not do

I do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing, do not send newsletters without your explicit consent, and do not use advertising trackers.

How long I keep data

Lesson-related data is kept while we work together and up to 2 years afterwards; invoices are kept 7 years as required by Dutch tax law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time — just e-mail hello@engelsmetlena.nl. You also have the right to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
